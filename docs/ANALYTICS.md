# Analytics Documentation

NeuroLLa measures performance in real time.

---

# Metrics

## Total Trades

Total executed trades.

---

## Win Rate

Formula:

Wins / Closed Trades

---

## Total PnL

Sum of all trade profits/losses.

---

## Profit Factor

Formula:

Gross Profit / Gross Loss

---

## Sharpe Ratio

Formula:

Mean Return / Std Return

Measures efficiency.

---

## Max Drawdown

Largest equity decline.

Measures survivability.
